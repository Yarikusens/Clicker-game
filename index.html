You have been chosen to lead the Mars colonization program! Your goal is to grow your colony as large as possible. Click the planet to encourage reproduction: every person earns you $50 per second. Spend that money on upgrades, so your population grows faster and your people work more efficiently

Call to action
is your gift for persuading colonists to start families, spreading the message far and wide; gives more babies per click
Birth rate
is the medical care and technology that help more babies be born into the colony; gives more babies passively
Economy
is the mines, farms and markets that make each colonist’s labor worth more than before; increases the value of work
Policy
is your iron-fisted code of laws and decrees that pressures colonists into having larger families; compels people to reproduce
